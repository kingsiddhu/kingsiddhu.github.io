My personal self-hosted cloud built from the ground up using Linux, Docker, and networking tools. This homelab runs my own services including DNS filtering, reverse proxying, game servers, remote access, automated printing, and LaTeX compilation. It is my playground for learning system administration, networking, cybersecurity, and infrastructure engineering.
Ever had to fill out the same boring document over and over? AutoDraft is my attempt to fix that. It uses AI, web scraping, and LaTeX templates to automatically create polished documents like reports, applications, and forms. Give it a template and some information, and it does the tedious writing for you.
A personal embedded systems project to build a modern take on the classic Walkman. Retro Dial combines an ESP32-S3, custom controls, audio playback, and a physical dial interface into a pocket-sized music player. Built from scratch to explore hardware design, communication protocols, and the joy of making unnecessary things the hard way.
AIDCore is my attempt at building a personal AI assistant that can actually do things instead of just answer questions. Built with local LLMs and LangGraph, it can interact with files, control Spotify, execute custom tools, and be expanded with new capabilities. Basically, a small AI brain running on my own machine.
A modular 3D flocking simulation built in Godot implementing the Boids algorithm for realistic autonomous movement. Features decentralized AI behaviors including separation, alignment, cohesion, and obstacle avoidance. Designed as a reusable framework with configurable resources and components for integrating intelligent agents into games, simulations, and interactive environments.
A neural network trained cancer information chatbot designed to provide accessible health education through natural language conversations. The application combines conversational AI and voice responses to help users explore cancer-related topics, symptoms, treatments, and scientific explanations through an interactive and personalized experience.
An AI-powered document analysis platform that simplifies complex bills by extracting information, explaining charges, identifying suspicious fees, and generating appeal letters. Billify uses a privacy-first architecture with local AI models for OCR and PII redaction before securely sending anonymized data to cloud models for deeper analysis.
A full-stack platform that transforms YouTube Shorts into a distraction-free educational learning database. HASS builds a local searchable index of 1,800+ curated educational videos using automated data pipelines, AI-powered categorization, keyword extraction, and a FastAPI backend for instant content discovery.
A 3D exploration adventure game developed by King Studios where players become underwater journalist Molly, discovering marine ecosystems, forgotten shipwrecks, and hidden ocean secrets. Combining exploration, photography-based gameplay, storytelling, and AI-assisted companionship, Project Marina delivers an immersive underwater experience focused on curiosity and discovery.

Every great journey begins not with a single step, but with the quiet willingness to see differently. Discovery is not reserved for the bold — it belongs to the curious, those who pause long enough to notice what others walk past.
The furthest places are not always on a map. Sometimes the horizon lives inside a conversation, a half-finished thought, or a moment of stillness between two loud things. Learning to look is the real voyage.
An idea doesn't announce itself. It arrives between tasks, in margins of notebooks, or in the half-awake hours before morning. The shape it takes depends entirely on the hands willing to hold it long enough.
Change rarely arrives with fanfare. It gathers in small habits, quiet choices, and stubborn patience. The revolutions worth living through are the ones you barely notice — until one day the world looks entirely different.
